Our Mission

Many Marine Species
Face Extinction

Plastic pollution, overfishing, habitat destruction, and climate change are threatening marine life around the world. As a result, many species face declining populations and an uncertain future.

Our mission is to:

Raise awareness about endangered marine species

Encourage action that doesn't harm marine life

Promote ocean conservation through everyday actions

Plastic Pollution

Marine animals often mistake floating plastic bags for food, which can cause injury, starvation, or death.

Overfishing

Every day, huge numbers of fish are removed from the ocean, putting pressure on marine ecosystems and fish populations.

Climate Change

Rising ocean temperatures cause corals to lose their color, leaving reefs weakened and vulnerable to damage.
